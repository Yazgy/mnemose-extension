# Mnemose — Browser Extension

A companion browser extension for [Mnemose](https://mnemose.com), the
movie / TV / anime tracker.

It lets you update your watch progress **from the page you're watching on**,
instead of opening Mnemose and searching for the title every time. The
extension recognises the title in your current tab and pushes the update to
your Mnemose library.

> **Status: pre-alpha.** Nothing is published yet. This repo currently holds
> the plan.

## The idea

**V1 — one click, everywhere.** The extension detects the work open in your
tab. From the toolbar popup you mark it watched or bump the episode, and it
syncs to Mnemose. Works on any site; every update is a manual action.

**V2 — automatic, site by site.** On sites you explicitly enable, the
extension advances your episode progress on its own as you watch. Opt-in,
added one site at a time.

## Planned support

Chrome and Firefox, from a single codebase.

## Requirements

A Mnemose account — the extension has no function on its own.
Main project: <https://github.com/Yazgy/Mnemose>
